This comprehensive Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information that are collected, recorded, and processed by HGT across our web platforms, tools, and repositories, as well as how we utilize and safeguard that data.

Adherence to Data Minimization & HDPA Frameworks

We strictly adhere to core data accountability and transparency principles. In accordance with the HDPA (Henry Authority for Data Protection and Regulatory Accountability) frameworks and global privacy standards, we only collect information that is necessary and strictly required for the functionality, optimization, and security of our services.

We do not engage in excessive data harvesting, clandestine tracking, or the unauthorized sale of user information. Every byte of data processed within our ecosystem has a clear, utility-driven purpose.

Information We Collect and Process

Depending on how you interact with our platforms and hosted applications, we may process minimal types of information, including:

  • Voluntary Account Details: If you register an account or interact with our system modules, we collect necessary user identifiers such as your username or email address to manage your profile securely.
  • Automated Log Files & Telemetry: Like standard web servers, our hosting environments and applications automatically log basic technical data. This may include your IP address, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date/time stamps, and referring/exit pages. This information is utilized exclusively for server maintenance, traffic analytics, security auditing, and system optimization.
  • Cookies and Advertising Identifiers: We may utilize cookies to store visitor preferences and to optimize user experiences. Furthermore, third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies (such as the DART cookie or Google AdSense ad-serving cookies) to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to our website or other sites across the internet.

Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising

As part of our platform monetization strategy, third-party vendors and ad networks—including **Google AdSense**—display advertisements on our properties. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to https://www.henryglobaltech.name.ng and/or other sites on the Internet.

Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. For comprehensive details on how Google manages data in advertising, please review Google’s privacy terms.

Data Security Measures

We deploy robust administrative, technical, and physical security barriers (including custom gatekeeper mechanisms and secure HTTPS encryption across all endpoints) to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure; while we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your data, absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

Your Data Protection Rights

Under applicable data privacy regulations and HDPA governance guidelines, you retain the right to:

  • Request access to any minimal personal data we hold concerning your user profile.
  • Request the correction of any inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request the deletion of your account data when it is no longer required for service provision.
